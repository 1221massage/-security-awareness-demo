🛡️ Safe Browsing Tips

1. Use strong passwords
Create long, unique passwords for different accounts.
2. Enable two-factor authentication
Add another layer of protection to important accounts.
3. Watch out for suspicious links
Check unexpected messages before opening links or attachments.
4. Keep your devices updated
Install security updates for your phone, computer and apps.
5. Never share verification codes
Do not share one-time passwords or account recovery codes with others.
